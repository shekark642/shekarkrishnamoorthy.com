The Industry Behind the Rave

A massive production stack runs underneath every mainstage moment. Here's who builds it.

Show & Lighting Software Software

What programmers and VJs actually run the show with.

MA Lighting (grandMA)

The dominant lighting console/software platform on major touring and festival mainstages worldwide.

Resolume

Industry-standard VJ software for real-time video mixing and projection mapping.

TouchDesigner

Node-based visual programming used to build the generative, audio-reactive visuals behind many headline sets.

Avolites

A long-standing rival lighting control platform, common across European festival circuits.

Pangolin (QuickShow/FB4)

The go-to software/hardware combo for laser show programming.

disguise (d3)

Media servers that drive massive LED walls and synchronize video with lighting and show control.

Lighting & Visual Fixtures Hardware

The moving heads, beams, and panels that actually paint the sky.

Claypaky

Italian manufacturer behind many of the moving-head fixtures seen on the largest festival mainstages.

Robe Lighting

Czech fixture maker with a huge footprint in touring and festival production.

GLP

Known for compact, high-output LED moving fixtures used in dense mainstage rigs.

Ayrton

French manufacturer of high-performance beam and wash fixtures.

Chauvet Professional

One of the largest lighting manufacturers globally, from club rigs up to festival scale.

Elation Professional

Another major fixture maker spanning club, touring, and festival markets.

Sound & Audio Systems Audio

The line arrays and subs that make a festival mainstage physically hit.

L-Acoustics

The K1/K2 line array systems are a fixture of major festival mainstages worldwide.

d&b audiotechnik

German manufacturer whose GSL/KSL systems are a common alternative to L-Acoustics at large events.

Meyer Sound

Long-established high-end line array and subwoofer systems used across touring and festivals.

Funktion-One

British system renowned in the dance music world specifically for its sound character.

Void Acoustics

Another prominent system in club and festival dance-music installs.

Clair Global

One of the largest touring audio production companies, providing systems and crews for major festivals.

Stage Design & Construction Stage

The structures the whole show gets built on.

TAIT

Builds some of the largest kinetic and structural mainstages in the industry, including major EDM festival stages.

Stageco

Belgian stage construction company with a long history building large-scale touring and festival stages.

All Access Staging & Productions

US-based staging and rigging company serving major festivals and tours.

Brilliant Stages

UK stage design and fabrication house known for elaborate custom mainstage builds.

Notable Performers Artists

Artists commonly associated with mainstage headline slots across the festival circuit — not a claim about any specific lineup.

Tiësto

One of the genre's most recognizable global headliners.

Martin Garrix

Regularly headlines mainstages at the largest festivals worldwide.

Carl Cox

A veteran of the techno/house scene with decades of festival headline sets.

Charlotte de Witte

A leading figure in modern techno's festival-mainstage era.

Above & Beyond

Long-running trance act known for large-scale, emotionally driven festival sets.

Eric Prydz

Known for the elaborate HOLO stage production built around his sets.

Production Tech & Cool Stuff Tech

The extra layer that turns a big stage into a spectacle.

CO2 & Pyro Cannons

Vendors like Le Maitre supply the CO2 jets, confetti, and pyrotechnics timed to drops.

LED Video Walls

Fine-pitch LED panels (e.g. from ROE Visual) form the massive backdrop screens behind DJs.

Drone Light Shows

Companies like Verge Aero choreograph swarms of drones as an alternative to fireworks.

RFID Wristbands

Cashless-payment and access-control wristband tech (e.g. Intellitix) used for festival entry and purchases.

Kinetic Stage Structures

Motorized stage elements that move, rotate, or transform mid-show.

Custom Trussing

The engineered aluminum rigging that every light, speaker, and screen actually hangs from.
